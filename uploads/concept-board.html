LingoJoy · Concept Board

Mascot & logo directions for a Duolingo-style language learning app — friendly, high-quality 3D characters, zero anime styling. “LingoJoy” is a placeholder name; every asset adapts to your real brand name.

1 · Mascot Candidates

Four creatures, four personalities. Each one is designed to react to the learner — that reactivity is what creates the Duolingo-style emotional hook.

Ember the Fox Recommended hero

Energetic, cheeky encourager. The streak-keeper who hypes every win.

  • Confetti jump after perfect lessons
  • Playful dares: “One more round? I bet you can!”
  • Guilty-cute sad face when a streak is about to die

Lumi the Polar Bear

Calm, cozy study buddy. Softens anxiety for new learners.

  • Gentle evening reminders instead of nagging pings
  • Reads “story mode” lessons aloud with you
  • Warm hug animation on course completion

Rio the Parrot

Chatty pronunciation coach — parrots repeat, so Rio repeats you.

  • Speaks the daily greeting phrase on app open
  • Mimicry mini-game: repeat after Rio for XP
  • Cheerful wing-flap when your accent scores well

Mochi the Otter

Curious collector — otters hold hands and treasures; Mochi holds your words.

  • Collects a “word pebble” for every new vocab learned
  • Floats on its back during review sessions
  • Shares pebbles with friends in friend-quests

2 · Logo Directions

A expressive lockup for marketing, and a minimal geometric system for product surfaces.

Direction A — Friendly Lockup

Mascot face in a rounded tile + rounded wordmark whose “o”s become speech bubbles. Instantly says conversation. Shown with placeholder name “LingoJoy”.

  • Best for: splash screens, store listing, socials

Direction B — Geometric Icon System

Flat two-tone marks for every mascot. Survives at 16 px, perfect for app icon, favicon and widget; each course-track can get its own animal.

  • Best for: app icon, widgets, course badges

3 · Engagement System

How the mascot keeps users coming back: emotions wired to app events, and a hero slot on the home screen.

Emotion System — the retention engine

One consistent character, mapped to every app event so the mascot reacts to the user:

  • Confetti jump → lesson complete / streak saved
  • Arms-up cheer → level up
  • Paw-on-chin think → hint offered on a hard exercise
  • Trophy hold → unit test passed / league win
  • Teary sad face → missed days, streak at risk
  • Wave + “Hello!” → returning after a break

In-app Placement

The mascot lives on the home screen, not just the logo — it celebrates above the progress bar, guards the streak flame, and mourns lost hearts.

  • Hero slot above daily-goal progress bar
  • Streak flame + hearts badges beside it
  • Tap the mascot → daily bonus phrase or joke

Verdict & Next Steps

  • Hero pick: Ember the fox. Warm orange pops against the mint/teal UI, the silhouette reads at icon size, and foxes feel clever-but-playful — distinct from Duolingo’s green owl while staying in the same friendly universe.
  • Sidekick: Rio the parrot for speaking/pronunciation features (parrots repeat — perfect metaphor).
  • Finalize the mascot in vector (SVG) with a 6–10 pose emotion set, then animate the 3 key reactions: celebrate, nudge, mourn-streak.
  • Build a mini style guide: palette chips above, rounded typeface, speech-bubble motif as the repeating brand shape.
  • Swap the “LingoJoy” wordmark for your app name and re-render the lockup.
