UX Principles

  • Loss-aversion loop: streak flame, hearts and league rank sit above the fold on every visit.
  • One-tap re-entry: “Continue Learning” card resumes the exact lesson — zero navigation cost.
  • Daily goal + gift: small achievable target (20 min) with a reward icon builds the endowment effect.
  • Mascot as coach: mascot greets on home, celebrates in lessons, appears in warm-up banners — emotional anchor.
  • Thumb-zone nav: 5 tabs — Home, Learning, Search, Rank, Profile; ≥44px targets, primary CTA full-width at the bottom.
  • Built-in TM↔EN dictionary: instant search over Turkmen–English entries keeps the app useful between lessons.
  • Rank league: weekly leaderboard with promotion/relegation zones turns study into a social game.
  • Frictionless onboarding: value prop in one sentence, social logins, <60s to first lesson.
  • Visible progress everywhere: path nodes, 60% bar, level meter, weekly chart — effort is never invisible.

Prototype is fully tappable — try the lesson: pick an answer, hit CHECK.
